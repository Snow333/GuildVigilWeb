Your five decisions

Recorded 2026-09-12.

Q1 · Milestone order M1 + M2 first — strike riders and stat_bonus. ~60% of the magic layer, and M2 needs no new content.
Q2 · Do riders scale? Flat for now — revisit when the level curve is retuned. Scaling is a balance call, not a wiring one.
Q3 · Starting skills Players allocate their own — at creation and level-up, so taking Arcana or Religion is a real choice. Auto-level-up takes Arcana for the Wizard and Religion for the Cleric, for role-play coherence.
Q4 · Unqualified scroll use Grey it out — consistent with every other readiness gate in the game.
Q5 · Native-tradition discount Yes — L + 1 for your own tradition, 2L + 1 for everyone else.

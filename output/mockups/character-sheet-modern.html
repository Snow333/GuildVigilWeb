On colour and accessibility. The five channels are distinguishable in the common forms of colour blindness because they differ in lightness as well as hue, and every coloured element is also labelled in text. Stacked bars carry a written key underneath; status pills carry a word; inert things additionally use a dashed border, so the “this does nothing” signal survives even in greyscale.

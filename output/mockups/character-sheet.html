Typography note. The character name and the small-caps section banners keep the Alegreya serif identity — they are short landmarks, not reading text. Everything you actually read repeatedly (stat labels, sources, numbers) is set in Atkinson Hyperlegible at 14–16px with tabular lining numerals and a slashed zero, so digits form clean columns. That split follows British Dyslexia Association guidance without losing the 1970s paper-sheet look. Your existing “readable type” setting already ships this font.

Five directions over byte-identical markup — every vignette below is stamped from one template; only a token set and a small labeled flourish layer differ. That is the Phase 3 thesis (reskin-by-stylesheet) working in miniature. Directions 1–3 follow the brief closely, 4 pushes the brief's spirit, 5 is the out-of-the-box pitch.

System rules that hold in every direction: the status set (Quiet/Restless/Threatened/Overrun, outcome bars) is fixed and never themed — validated #0ca30c · #fab219 · #ec835a · #d03b3b, always paired with its text label, never color alone.

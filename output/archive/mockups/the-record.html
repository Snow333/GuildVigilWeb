What makes this hard: every line has equal weight, so nothing stands out. The attack and the damage it caused are two unrelated rows. Names repeat on the far left but so does everything else, so there is no column to scan down. 3+-3=0 prints a double sign. casts spell 14 shows a database id. Ticks are cosmetic — 15, 18, 21 mean nothing to a reader.

Seven changes, each doing one job:
Rounds, not ticks. Grouped under headers with the tick range kept as a small caption. Actor column. Names right-aligned in a fixed 74px column, so you scan down one edge to follow one character.
Damage attaches to its cause. The result is indented under the attack that produced it, not a separate row. Outcome as a word in a bordered pill. Hit / Critical / Miss / Slain — shape and text, never colour alone.
Misses collapse. Consecutive whiffs fold into “3 misses”, expandable. The four miss lines were 24% of this fight. Spell names, not ids. casts spell 14 becomes casts Bless.
Clean arithmetic. 3+-3=0 becomes 3 − 3 = 0, tabular figures so digits line up. Filters. “Kills” or “Our turns” turns a 25-line fight into the 4 lines you wanted.
One real bug this surfaced. The record prints casts spell 14 — a raw database id. Since the potions shipped in M3 route through the same casting path, every potion your party drinks will currently read casts spell 208. The consumables feature is invisible in the log it is supposed to appear in.

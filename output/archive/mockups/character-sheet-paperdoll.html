Character sheet — D + E blended, with a paperdoll

Three ways to combine the dashboard (D) and the inspector (E) around a paperdoll. The paperdoll's job here is not decoration — it is to make empty and underserved slots visible at a glance. Three states, each carrying shape and text so they survive greyscale: solid = working, dashed = empty, hatched with a badge = filled but not pulling its weight.

Grouping by what it improves rather than by slot answers the question a player actually asks — “what is making my AC 21?” — without needing a separate ledger. The third group is the honest one: everything carried that produces no effect.

The underserved state is the new idea here. Empty slots are easy; the harder signal is a slot that looks filled but is not pulling its weight — a weapon whose magic the engine ignores, or an off-class item taking the proficiency penalty from brief #23. Those get a hatched fill and a ! badge, so a fully-equipped hero can still show you where the slack is.

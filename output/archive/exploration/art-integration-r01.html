D · NOTES FOR REVIEW

  • Load a real image (top of page) — an anchor sheet or a batch-1 generation — and judge every grade with it. The stand-in figure only proves the plumbing.
  • Grades compose by data: faction from allegiance, condition from HP state, frame from rank. Wounded here shows base+wounded; Krath-graded enemies would show base+krath (+elite if ranked).
  • Sketch-pending silhouette is the proposed fallback wherever art doesn't exist yet (8 of 12 hero sheets after batch 1). Alternative: hold portrait wiring until all 12 pass. Which?
  • Muster sheet assumes the recruit flow gains an ancestry+gender choice sealed by wax. Where existing heroes (already-saved campaigns) get their ancestry is a system question for the interview, not this mock.
  • Flat mode (toggle, top) — portrait + frame stay, tilt/tape/grain/grades drop, wounded/lost desat stays label-paired. Check it matches your reading of the bible §5 decision.
  • Busts are square crops from the 3:2 sheets for now (object-fit center-top on the front view). Per-sheet crop tuning is a build-tool concern, not a UI one.

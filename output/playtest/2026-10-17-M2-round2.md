# Playtest — M2 round 2 (portraits, abilities, items, mid-level board)

**Date:** 2026-10-17 · **Build:** `main` · **Saves broken?** No — old saves load.
Run the **Guild Vigil** desktop shortcut; it builds latest `main`.

---

## What's new

**Every ancestry has a face.** The 8 missing busts are in (Elf, Dwarf, Halfling,
Gnome × woman/man), so all 12 ancestry × gender portraits are real art. The
silhouette "awaiting field sketch" placeholder should now never appear on a hero.

**The quest board keeps going.** 41 new jobs covering levels 6–15. The board used
to have one job above level 7 in the whole game, which is why it went thin around
week 17. It also now puts jobs you *can* take at the top, and makes sure you see
both a camp fight and a dungeon rather than five dungeons.

**Magic weapons are priced by their magic.** A +2 dagger used to cost 16g while a
+2 longsword cost 120g, because the price came off the base weapon. Now the rune
carries the value: masterwork ~40g, +1 ~115g, +2 ~255g, +3 ~630g, roughly the
same on any weapon. The Staff is no longer free.

**Scroll of Raise Dead was casting Scorching Ray.** It's now the Scroll of Breath
of Life and actually heals a dying hero. (The real Raise Dead spell does nothing
in the engine — see "what I chose" below.)

**Feats you pick in a fight are worth picking.** Power Attack and friends used to
make a fighter *worse*. That's fixed.

## Known rough edges

- **Power Attack is still not in the fight plan automatically.** You have to place
  it yourself in the loadout editor. Measured: auto-using it makes a *low-level*
  party worse (see below), and I'd rather you choose it than have it quietly
  hurt you.
- **29 of 52 scrolls point at spells the engine can't execute** (Haste, Fly,
  Dispel Magic, Mirror Image…). They'd do nothing if read. Not fixed — it's a
  bigger content job.
- **No magic weapon is actually purchasable yet.** The shop's only one
  (Longsword +1) is gated behind a building level that never shows. The new
  prices currently only matter for loot and selling.
- **Armour +N prices still use the old multiplier** and may be odd in the same way.
- **Ranger and Bard still have zero class feats.** The "ranged ranger" flagship
  from M2 is not built.

## What I chose, and why (reverse any of it)

**Scroll of Raise Dead → Scroll of Breath of Life.** Raise Dead (spell 106) is a
healing row with no dice and a `resurrect` flag nothing reads — pointing the
scroll at it would have moved the lie rather than fixed it. There's also no
permanent death in the game yet for it to undo. Breath of Life is a real 4d8 heal
that wakes a dying hero, so the scroll now does what its name says.

**Abilities ride a normal attack instead of replacing it.** Power Attack measured
at **45–53% of a plain strike** — it cost you three of every four swings to add
one damage die. Now it adds its die to a normal attack and waits a couple of
seconds before it can fire again. Measured at 109–116% of a plain strike.

**Enemies got this too, so the game is slightly harder:** autopilot completion
96.3% → 95.0%, wipes 3.3% → 4.4%. Classed enemies were paying the same broken
tax, and now their signature moves land properly.

## The interesting thing I found

You asked whether Power Attack starts paying off as enemies get tougher. It does,
and here's the shape of it — measured against the real enemy rows at each level:

| Party/enemy level | Enemy HP | Damage wasted on corpses | Power Attack verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 8 | 42% | **hurts** |
| 3 | 24 | 29% | neutral |
| 5 | 44 | 21% | slight gain |
| 7 | 55 | 15% | **5–6% faster clears** |
| 8 | 120 | — | **win rate 50% → 55%** |

The reason is that an attack is a burst of two swings, and the burst **stops when
the target dies and doesn't turn to the next enemy**. A bigger first hit kills the
goblin sooner and throws the second swing away. As enemies get beefier that stops
mattering.

Which points at something bigger: **at level 1 a party wastes 42% of all its
damage hitting things that are already dead** — no feats involved. Making attacks
carry on to the next enemy would lift every hero in the game. I haven't done it;
it changes every fight and deserves its own pass. Say the word.

# Playtest — M4: two parties, exploration, outposts

**Date:** 2026-10-20 · **Build:** `main` · **Saves broken?** No — old saves load.
Run the **Guild Vigil** shortcut; it builds latest `main`.

---

## The three new things

**1. A second party.** Build the **Barracks** and the guild can keep more
heroes than it can march. Ten beds → a second band marches on its own job.

| Barracks | Heroes you can keep | Bands |
|---|---|---|
| none | 4 | 1 |
| level 1 | 6 | 1 |
| **level 2** | **10** | **2** |
| level 3 | 15 | 3 |

That ladder is not a number I picked — the Barracks has said "hero cap 10"
since long before this milestone, and 10 is your 6+4.

**2. The map starts dark.** It opens along the roads you have actually
walked. A job in the dark shows as a pale ring with a **?** — you know
something is out there, not what. Build the **Watchtower** and you see
further *around everywhere you have already been*, not just from now on.

**3. Outposts.** A resource site you hold is a staging post. A party heading
to a job in that region sets out from the site instead of from Haven.

---

## What I corrected from M3

**Hiring was capped by the wrong building.** M3 used the Guild Hall, but the
Guild Hall is how many heroes **march** and the Barracks is how many you can
**keep**. Fused together, you could never have a reserve — so a second party
was impossible. That is why the Town screen now says "4 of 6" counting beds,
and tells you to upgrade the Barracks rather than the hall.

## What to try

1. **Build a Barracks to level 2, hire up to ten, and run two jobs in one
   week.** The M4 question is yours to answer: **is this fun, or busywork?**
2. **Look at the map on week 1**, then again after three or four missions.
   Does the opening-up feel like exploring, or like admin?
3. **Build the Watchtower** and look again. Old roads should widen.
4. **Take a resource site, then take a job in that same region.** The ETA
   should drop. Does that read as a reward for holding ground?
5. **Try running the second band with your weaker heroes.** Does a B-team
   feel useful or just risky?

## Numbers I measured

**Outposts** — over 40 campaigns, every region that had a job posted:

| Region band | Jobs it helped | Typical saving |
|---|---|---|
| L1 | 7 of 32 | 41% |
| L2 | 16 of 50 | 37% |
| L3 | 24 of 75 | 36% |
| L4 | 16 of 62 | 42% |

So an outpost helps about **a quarter to a third** of the jobs in its region,
and when it does it takes roughly **a third off the trip**. It does not always
help because the site has to actually be closer than Haven — that is correct,
not a shortfall.

**Fog** — sight is **3 tiles** base, **6** with a Watchtower, **9** at level 2.
On a fresh guild that is 29 → 446 → 732 lit tiles with no extra walking.

## Known rough edges

- **The sight ranges 3 / 6 / 9 are a guess.** Not playtested. They live in one
  constant and are a one-line change if the map feels too blind or too open.
- **There is no UI for assigning heroes to bands yet.** The engine supports it
  (move a hero between bands, leave one at the hall), but the Town screen does
  not show it. Right now bands fill automatically as you hire.
- **A site can never be lost.** No raids, no upkeep.
- **19 of 43 building effects still do nothing** — Infirmary, Training Grounds,
  crafting, enchanting. They are greyed and tagged **"Not working yet"**, and
  the screen never recommends one.
- **Region names stay readable in the dark** on purpose — they are news from
  the board, not survey. Say if that feels wrong.
- A region label can sit on top of a map pin. That predates this work.

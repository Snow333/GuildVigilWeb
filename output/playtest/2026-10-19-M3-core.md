Run the **Guild Vigil** shortcut; it builds latest `main`.

---

## The loop M3 adds

1. **Secure a resource site** out on the map — 12 of them, timber to mithril.
2. **Collect** what it produces, every week, for free, forever.
3. **Spend** resources + gold on a building.
4. **Get something you can see.**

| Building | What you get |
|---|---|
| **Guild Hall** | **A 5th, then a 6th hero in the party** |
| **Tavern** | Recruits turn up at all — and better ones as it grows |
| **Blacksmith / Store** | The shelf gets wider: 6 → 8 → 10 things on sale |
| **Market** | Your loot sells for 80%, then 90%, then full price |
| **Library** | Heroes level 5–10% faster |
| **Stables** | Missions travel 25–50% faster |

## What I need you to decide

**1. Pick a town screen.** Three variants, deliberately different:

| | Approach |
|---|---|
| **A — The Ledger** | All 18 buildings, one line each, grouped, with filters |
| **B — Do This Next** | Your 3 best upgrades up top, the other 15 folded away |
| **C — The Town Plan** | A clickable map of what unlocks what |

**My pick is B.** 18 buildings × 3 levels is a wall, and B is the only one that
answers "what should I do now?" without reading everything. A is the best
reference view and could live behind a "show all" toggle on B. C is the
prettiest and explains the *shape* of the town best, but it's the most work and
the least useful once you know the layout.

**2. Is the week-one shop nerf OK?** This is the one change I'd expect you to
push back on. Shop stock is gated on building level, and that gate was never
switched on before — so every level-1 item was on sale from the start.

- **Before:** ~60 items available in week one.
- **Now:** ~31 — you start with a Store and a Smith, and that's it.
- The **Wizard Guild's 40 scrolls** and the **Market** are now things you build.

I think that's correct — it's the milestone working — but it does mean no
scrolls at all until you invest. Say the word and I'll start the Wizard Guild
at level 1 too.

## What to try (once there's a screen)

1. Take a **timber** site, then watch timber arrive weekly. Does free income
   feel good, or just slow?
2. Save for the **Guild Hall**. Is a 5th hero worth what it costs?
3. Hire from the **Tavern**. Does a 5-hero party feel different, or just safer?
4. Check the **Blacksmith** before and after upgrading — can you tell?

## Known rough edges

- **No town screen.** See above.
- **22 of 43 building effects do nothing yet** — Infirmary, Training Grounds,
  Watchtower, the crafting and enchanting trades, Barracks' hero cap. They're
  marked **"not working yet"** in the data so the UI can grey them out. I'd
  rather show you an honest blank than charge you for a lie.
- **Securing a site isn't a real mission yet.** The engine banks a secured
  site, but there's no "go and take it" quest — that's the next piece.
- **The Guild Hall costs no gold**, only timber and stone, because its gold
  price is authored as 0 in the data. I left it: it makes the headline upgrade
  something you go and *mine*, not something you buy. Easy to change.
- **Hero cap is not enforced** — Barracks says "room for 10" and nothing reads
  it. Party size is the real cap right now.

## What I measured before building any of it

Four things were already written down and read by nothing:

| Authored | Was consumed by |
|---|---|
| 18 buildings, costs, prerequisites, per-level effects | one line, for a shop label |
| Every region's list of what it produces | nothing |
| 45 of 105 shop rows needing level 2–3 | nothing — behind a hardcoded skip |
| Quest reputation rewards | announced, then thrown away |

M3 gives all four a consumer. Three bugs turned up on the way: the shop went
**empty** when real levels arrived, upgrading a shop was **invisible** (the
shelf never widened), and resource sites **piled onto the map edge**, two of
them on the same tile. All three are fixed and pinned by tests.

# Playtest — M2: signature classes and skills that matter

**Date:** 2026-10-18 · **Build:** `main` · **Saves broken?** No — old saves load.
Run the **Guild Vigil** desktop shortcut; it builds latest `main`.

---

## What's new

**Ranger and Bard exist now.** Both classes previously gave you *nothing* when
you levelled them — no feats at all, and five empty class-feat slots by level 10.

| Class | What it does in a fight |
|---|---|
| **Ranger** | Shoots to control: slows, shoves, frightens and trips from range |
| **Bard** | Sings: **the whole party attacks better**, and the enemy fights worse |
| **Wizard** | Touch-casting: +1 to hit with spells, +1d4 on touch spells, a force ward |
| **Fighter** | Unchanged, but his feats now *help* instead of hurting |

**Four more skills matter on a mission.** Arcana, Religion, Survival and
Deception each do something you can see in the mission report:

- **Arcana** — rune-sealed hazards that lockpicks and brute force are bad at
- **Religion** — wards undead so they swing and defend worse (living foes, no effect)
- **Survival** — foraging on a rest heals the party more
- **Deception** — a feint opens the fight with the enemy caught off-guard

**Feats are worth taking.** The earlier fix means a feat you pick adds to a
normal attack instead of replacing it.

## What to try

1. **Found a party with a Bard in it and watch one fight.** Can you see the
   song land? Everyone should hit noticeably more often while it's up.
2. **Build a Ranger and give it the shot feats as you level.** Does "keep them
   at a distance" actually read as a style, or does it just look like archery?
3. **Build a Wizard and stand it next to things.** Touch spells should hit
   harder than ranged ones. Is that fun, or is standing a wizard in melee just
   a way to get it killed?
4. **Train Arcana or Religion on somebody and send them into a dungeon.** Did
   the report ever tell you the skill mattered?
5. **Play 10 missions and ask the M2 question:** can you tell your four heroes
   apart by watching a fight?

## Known rough edges

- **Only the Bard's songs go into the fight plan by themselves.** Everything
  else — Power Attack, the Ranger's shots, the Wizard's ward — you place in the
  loadout editor. That is deliberate and measured: auto-using the others makes
  a party *worse* (see below), but it does mean a new Ranger does nothing
  special until you set it up. Tell me if that feels like homework.
- **The Rogue is the thinnest flagship.** Sneak Attack works and is automatic,
  but it only has one real active (Poison Weapon).
- **The Wizard still has 21 feats that do nothing** — Counterspell, Familiar,
  Spell Mastery and friends. I fixed the three that could be fixed without
  per-feat engine work and left the rest honest rather than fake.
- **Bard's Inspire Competence grants nothing** on purpose: its only honest
  effect is a Performance bonus and nothing reads Performance yet.
- Dungeons are slightly easier again now the four skills help: completion
  95.0% → 96.5%, wipes 4.6% → 3.3%. That's back to roughly where it was before
  the ability-model change.

## What I chose, and why (reverse any of it)

**Only party songs auto-fire.** This took three measurements:

| Fight plan | Autopilot completion |
|---|---|
| no abilities planned | 95.0% |
| every ability planned | 89.8% |
| every non-strike ability planned | 90.4% |
| **only party songs planned** | **95.0%** (and +1.7 party win rate) |

I assumed "a buff can't be wasted, so buffs are safe to auto-use". That was
wrong and the harness said so — a ward or a poison coating costs a whole
action for very little. A song is the exception because it helps everyone at
once.

**Cleave is still yours to spend.** Per your call, the attack burst still
stops when a target dies rather than carrying on. It's recorded in three
places so nobody "fixes" it and gives carry-through away for free.

**Deception, not Trickery.** Trickery isn't a skill in this game; Deception is.

# Playtest — M3: the town grows

**Date:** 2026-10-19 · **Build:** `main` · **Saves broken?** No — old saves load.
Run the **Guild Vigil** shortcut; it builds latest `main`.

**M3 is playable end to end.** You can earn resources and spend them.

---

## The new loop

1. **Town Hub → Town & Upgrades** — the new screen.
2. **Send a party to take a resource site.** 12 of them on the map.
3. **A secured site pays you every week, forever.**
4. **Spend resources + gold on a building.** The screen tells you what you get.

| Building | What it gives you |
|---|---|
| **Guild Hall** | **A 5th, then a 6th hero** |
| **Tavern** | Recruits turn up at all — better ones as it grows |
| **Blacksmith / Store** | The shelf widens: 6 → 8 → 10 items on sale |
| **Market** | Loot sells for 80%, then 90%, then full price |
| **Library** | Heroes level 5–10% faster |
| **Stables** | Missions travel 25–50% faster |

## What to try

1. **Open Town & Upgrades on week 1.** It should say nothing is ready yet and
   point you at "Show all 18". Is that a dead end or a to-do list?
2. **Take the grain or timber camp** (both level 1 — a starting party wins
   these comfortably). Then watch the resource arrive each week.
3. **Save for the Guild Hall**, hire a 5th hero from the Tavern, and send six
   out. Does a bigger party feel different, or just safer?
4. **Upgrade the Blacksmith** and look at the shop before and after.
5. **Try a level-3 site** (stone, iron) with a fresh party. You should lose —
   tell me if losing there feels unfair rather than "come back later".

## The one thing I'd expect you to push back on

**Stone exists in only one place — Gravel Downs, a level-3 site.** The Guild
Hall needs 12 stone. So **the 5th hero is gated behind levelling to ~L3**,
not something you can rush in week one.

I think that's a decent goal to play toward. But it does mean the headline
M3 upgrade is a mid-early milestone, not an opening move. Easy to change: I
can add stone to a second region, or drop the Guild Hall's stone cost.

**Also still standing from last note** (you didn't object, so I kept it): the
week-one shop is ~31 items instead of ~60. Scrolls and the Market are now
things you build. One line reverses it.

## Known rough edges

- **22 of 43 building effects do nothing yet** — Infirmary, Training Grounds,
  Watchtower, hero caps, crafting, enchanting. They are **greyed and tagged
  "Not working yet"** on the screen. You can still build them; you just
  shouldn't, and the screen says so rather than taking your money quietly.
- **The screen never recommends one of those.** The three picks are ranked
  cheapest-first and skip any upgrade whose effect is dead.
- **Barracks is a dead end today** — it is unwired AND it is the prerequisite
  for Training Grounds and Watchtower, which are also unwired.
- **A site can't be lost once taken.** No raids, no upkeep. If holding ground
  should cost something, that's a design call I haven't made.
- **The ledger shows one row per building**, not the full 3-level ladder per
  building — the sim only exposes the next level. Guild Hall's full ladder is
  shown separately because it's the headline.
- **No resource icons**, just names and numbers.

## What I measured

**Site difficulty**, a fresh level-1 party, 12 runs each:

| Site level | Won |
|---|---|
| L1 — grain, timber | 12/12 |
| L2 — clay, fish | 12/12 |
| L3+ — stone, iron, mithril | 0/12 |

The garrison comes from each region's authored danger level, so that ordering
falls out of the content rather than me tuning it.

# M2 feedback round: your 8 requests (2026-10-16)

**How to play:** the **Guild Vigil** Desktop shortcut (it rebuilds).
**Saves:** ⚠ **start a new campaign** to see the new board and the founding spellbooks. Old saves still
load, but stash items show their real (often lower) sell price, and the Marshal's letter shows again.

## What's new

| You asked | What you'll see |
|---|---|
| Easy early quests | Weeks 1–4 always have **at least 2 easy jobs** (5 new small fights, e.g. "Rats in the Granary Cellar"). A level-1 party wins every one (measured 40/40). |
| Bigger quest board | Holds **up to 10**, with **4–6 new jobs each week**. Each row starts with **easy / fair / hard**. |
| "We'll look into it." | Answering the Marshal posts a **sealed commission** at the top of the board: "Guard the Last Granary", bonus pay, 4-week expiry. |
| Magic items all "+1 to every strike" | Cards now say what items really do: "+2 to hit · 1d6 piercing", "AC +3 (Dex max +2)", "+1 to all saves". Things not working yet are **greyed**. |
| Cleric picks one spell | Clerics now get **every** divine spell they can cast, and choose what to **prepare** (one per slot). |
| Choosing caster priorities | New **spells** tab on casters: prepare spells, then set the **casting order** and **when / at whom** for each. |
| Laggy scrolling in Edge | There was **no frame cap**. The desk background and the sheets lifting under the mouse made the page repaint while scrolling. Fixed. In tests, redraw work during scrolling fell about 8× on the dispatch screen. |
| Bust portraits | Prompt pack ready: `output/art/bust-prompts-batch2.md` (about 20 min in ChatGPT). |

## What to try (about 20 minutes)

1. **New campaign.** Click **"We'll look into it."**, open the board, and find the sealed commission.
2. **Play weeks 1–4** with only easy jobs. Do the wins feel earned, or too easy?
3. **Open Mira (Cleric) → spells.** Prepare Harm instead of Bless, run a fight, and check she casts it.
4. **Elandra (Wizard) → spells.** Move a spell to the top and change "when" to "when an enemy is close".
5. **Equip a magic cloak or weapon** from loot or the shop. Do the cards make sense?
6. **Scroll the dispatch screen while a fight plays,** then scroll the after-action report. Smoother?

## Known rough edges

- **"Hard" can be wrong.** Labels come from level ranges, not win rates. Skeleton Crypt says hard, but a
  level-1 party wins it about 59 in 60.
- **The board thins out later.** From about week 17 it often shows only 1–3 jobs, because there aren't enough mid-level
  quests yet. More quest content will fix this.
- **Sheets don't lift under the mouse while you scroll.** They lift again a moment after you stop.
- Holy, Unholy and Keen on weapons are greyed (the engine can't run them yet).
- Scroll of Raise Dead casts Scorching Ray (content bug, flagged).
- Some prices are odd: a Dagger +2 costs less than a Masterwork Longsword, and staffs cost 0g.

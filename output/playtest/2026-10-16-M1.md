# M1 playtest — the whole loop, thin (2026-10-16)

**How to play:** double-click the **Guild Vigil** shortcut on your Desktop. It rebuilds and opens the game.

**Saves:** ⚠ **Start a new campaign.** Old saves may load, but heroes made before M1 don't have
ancestry stats and the new XP numbers.

## What's new

| | What changed |
|---|---|
| **Founding** | All 10 classes can be picked. Each shows a one-line play style. **Ancestry now changes stats**: two +2 boosts, one −2 flaw, and base hit points, shown on each ancestry tile. |
| **Levelling pace** | Every hero gets the full XP from a quest (it used to be split four ways). First level-up comes in about 5 weeks. Higher levels need more XP. |
| **Level-up** | Casters **learn new spells** each level, and new spells go **straight into the fight plan**. The screen only lists things you can actually pick. |
| **Auto level up** | One click does the whole level. It's on the hero's level-up tab, beside each hero in the town roster, and "Auto level up all" is in town Orders and on the after-action screen. |
| **After-action** | A new **"What happened"** line and a per-hero table: damage dealt, damage taken, healing, kills, times downed, died. |
| **Quest names** | Real names ("The Burned Granary") instead of "quest #3". |
| **Fixes** | Warlocks can cast (they had 0 energy). Monks punch with their class die using DEX. Weak-STR heroes (wizards, bards) now do real melee damage: a dice bug made it NaN. |

## What to try (about 20 minutes)

1. **Found a mixed guild.** Try a Monk, a Warlock and a Bard. Does ancestry feel like it matters now?
2. **Play 5–6 weeks.** Is the first level-up too slow, too fast, or about right?
3. **Level a caster by hand once.** Is the spell list readable? Is it clear what each spell does?
4. **Then use Auto level up.** Is it what you'd want most of the time?
5. **Read the after-action table.** Can you tell who carried the fight in under ten seconds?

**Shortcuts:** press **`** (the key left of 1) for the dev panel: +gold, +XP, +level, heal, reveal map.
Press **✎ Note** (bottom right) any time something feels wrong. Tell me "collect my notes" afterwards.

## Known rough edges

- **Abilities aren't used on their own.** A Fighter's Power Attack and similar sit in the fight plan
  only if you add them yourself. Using them every turn lost most fights (measured), so when to use them is an M2 job.
- **Debuff spells** (Sleep, Fear, Grease) can be learned, but you must add them to the fight plan yourself.
- **Feats:** only about 30 of 227 do anything yet. The picker now hides the rest, so some levels may offer
  only a few choices, and **Rangers and Bards get no class feats at all** yet.
- The playback screen heading still says "quest <name>".

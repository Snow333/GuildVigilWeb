# UI, art pipeline & the record

The desk-grammar LAW stays in CLAUDE.md; these are the traps that law does not cover.

---

- ⚠ **THE LEVEL-UP WIZARD HAS A REQUIRED FEAT STEP, AND IT BROKE THE E2E.** The skill table and Commit button do not render until every fillable slot is filled, so a spec that jumps straight to `+` hangs for 5 minutes waiting for a button that can never appear. `e2e/core-loop.spec.ts` fills slots via **`button[data-feat-offer]`** — match the ATTRIBUTE, never feat NAMES, which are content. Also: `"Fighter → 2"` is an e2e-pinned string and replacing the arrow with `-&gt;` breaks it (#22 did this and the e2e caught it).
- ⚠ **THE PAPERDOLL ART PIPELINE, AND WHY ITS BACKGROUND KEY IS SHAPED THE WAY IT IS.** `art/heroes/*-figure-01.png` → `pnpm figures` (`tools/build-figures.mjs`) → inline webp data URIs, same zero-image-assets law as portraits. The key is a **flood fill from the image border against a POSITION-DEPENDENT reference** (bilinear from the border pixels on each row/column), and three simpler approaches failed in ways worth not repeating: a luminance threshold punched holes through dark hair (dark hair and a dark background share a brightness — the darkest hair pixels measured **distance 0.0** from the field); a single corner reference left pale slabs behind figures on the vignettes that diffusion models paint; and spreading by LOCAL similarity walked up pale robes and **dissolved four figures from the waist down**. ⚠ **A low clear-ratio means REGENERATE THE ART, never raise `FIELD_TOLERANCE`** — a clean figure clears 65–80% of its frame, a painted backdrop plateaus near 40% however high you push it, and chasing it with tolerance is what ate the figures. The tool now measures and names suspect files. ⚠ **Never say "character sheet" or "concept art" in a `gen-figures.py` prompt** — both pull SDXL to a turnaround sheet with two figures in frame.
- ⚠ **`tests/ui/figures.test.ts` FAILED TO CATCH THAT REGRESSION THREE WAYS, all generalisable.** It sampled 25/40/55% height while the damage was at 65–80%; extended to 82%, "longest opaque run" then failed a HEALTHY figure because at ankle height a standing figure is two narrow shapes; and `SAMPLES` was a hand-written list of four keys that did not include the one broken figure. **Derive the sample set from the data (`Object.keys(figures)`), sample where the failure actually lives, and pick a measure that separates damage from legitimate shape.**
- ⚠ **`position: fixed` IS NOT VIEWPORT-RELATIVE UNDER THE DESK GRAMMAR.** The sheet cards carry a slight `rotate` for the paper look, and any ancestor transform creates a new containing block — a tooltip positioned at 591px against a container at 590px rendered in the wrong place with correct arithmetic. **Portal overlays to `document.body`** (`src/ui/components/ItemTooltip.tsx`). Also: a positioning effect keyed on a REF never re-runs, because a ref does not re-render — the panel shipped with `left: 0px; top: 0px`. Use a callback ref stored in state.
- ⚠ **THE RECORD'S PROSE AND ITS LAYOUT ARE DIFFERENT CONTRACTS.** `BeatLine.text` is pinned by the contract snapshot and read by the harness; `BeatLine.parts` (optional) carries the structured actor/pill/target/math/effect fields the record's column layout needs. `src/ui/beats/record.ts` owns GROUPING (rounds, damage folded under its blow, tallies) and is free to change; `interpret.ts` owns WHAT A BEAT SAYS and is not. ⚠ **Beats carry DISAMBIGUATED display names** (`namesFromStream` numbers duplicates: "Goblin ɪ" … "Goblin ɪᴠ") while spawn facts carry the plain name — keying anything off `spawn.name` misses every duplicate, which is exactly how the hp bar silently never drew.
